## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

Scale AI founder and Meta’s AI chief Alexandr Wang has some pretty direct advice for teenagers growing up with AI.

He thinks learning to build with these tools early could matter the same way learning computers early mattered for people like Bill Gates and Mark Zuckerberg.

The idea is not just to use AI for answers, but to actually make things with it, test ideas, and get comfortable building from a young age.

For a 13-year-old today, Wang basically sees AI as one of the biggest skills worth growing up with.
