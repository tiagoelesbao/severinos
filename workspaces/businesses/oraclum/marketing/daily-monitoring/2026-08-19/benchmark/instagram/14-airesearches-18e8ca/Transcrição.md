## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

A tweet from 2012 saying “STOP CHAT GPT!!” is suddenly going viral because, at first glance, it looks like someone mentioned ChatGPT a full decade before it launched.

But there’s a much simpler explanation. The account appears to be Indonesian, and “gpt” is commonly used online as shorthand for “ga penting,” which roughly means “not important” or “pointless.”

So the tweet was likely just telling someone to stop sending pointless messages, not somehow predicting ChatGPT years before OpenAI even existed.

Still, the wording lining up this perfectly more than 10 years later is a pretty wild coincidence.
