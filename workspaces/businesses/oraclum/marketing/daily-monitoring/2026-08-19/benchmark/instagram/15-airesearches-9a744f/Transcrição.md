## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

🧓🏻 A grandson gave his 82-year-old grandmother a surprise she never saw coming.

Using AI, he placed her directly into scenes from one of her favorite Clint Eastwood Westerns, then quietly played the edited movie on the TV without telling her what he had done.

Her reaction is completely real. She slowly realizes that the person appearing inside the movie is actually her, and her shocked response makes the whole moment even better.

A few years ago, creating something like this would have taken skilled VFX work, rotoscoping, and professional editing software. Now, AI is making this kind of personalized movie magic dramatically easier to create.

Would you do this for someone in your family?
