# Transcrição Estruturada (Carrossel) — The em dash had a perfectly respectable life until AI showed up.

**Plataforma:** ig-carousel  ·  **Slides:** 2

## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

The em dash had a perfectly respectable life until AI showed up.

Now you use one — and suddenly everyone assumes ChatGPT wrote the whole thing.

A beautiful piece of punctuation ruined by association.

Justice for the em dash. ✊
