## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

A robot just passed one of the internet’s most ironic challenges: proving it’s “not a robot.”

CAPTCHAs were designed to separate humans from automated systems, but this experiment took a different approach. Instead of relying only on software, a robotic arm physically interacted with the verification interface and completed the task much like a person would.

That doesn’t mean CAPTCHA is suddenly useless. Modern verification systems look at far more than a single click, including behavior patterns, device signals, and other clues designed to detect automation.

Still, the experiment raises a funny question: if robots can increasingly copy the way humans interact with computers, how long will “I’m not a robot” tests actually work?
