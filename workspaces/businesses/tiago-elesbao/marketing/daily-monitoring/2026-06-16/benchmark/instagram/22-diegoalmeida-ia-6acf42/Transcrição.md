## 1. Transcribed Text:

*   **Attribution:** Diego Almeida
*   **Handle:** diegoalmeida.ia

## 2. Visual Elements, Colors, and Mood:

*   **Visual Elements:**
    *   The primary visual is a photograph of a large group of graduating students in academic regalia (caps and gowns).
    *   Many students are wearing black caps and gowns, with some having colorful trim on their gowns.
    *   One student in the foreground, wearing a red and black robe, is prominently taking a selfie with a smartphone.
    *   The background is filled with more students, creating a sense of scale and a collective event.
    *   There are navigation arrows on the left and right sides of the image, suggesting it's part of a carousel or slideshow.
    *   Small white dots at the bottom indicate the current position within a series of content.
    *   A small profile picture and name/handle are located in the top right corner, indicating the source or author.

*   **Colors:**
    *   The dominant colors are **black** (from the caps and gowns) and **red** (from the robe of the selfie-taking student and some gown trims).
    *   There are also accents of **yellow** and **blue/purple** from the colorful gown trims.
    *   The background is slightly desaturated, with the focus on the students.
    *   The text is primarily **white** against a dark background, ensuring readability.
    *   The profile picture uses natural skin tones and a dark background.

*   **Mood:**
    *   The mood is **informative and somewhat serious**, due to the news-like headline and body text discussing educational reform and changes.
    *   However, the image itself, with the graduating students and the selfie, introduces an element of **youthfulness, celebration, and modernity**.
    *   The contrast between the formal academic setting and the casual act of taking a selfie creates a slight sense of **juxtaposition**.
    *   Overall, it aims for a mood that is **authoritative in its information but relatable through its visual representation of students.**

## 3. Design Triggers:

*   **Scarcity/Urgency (Implied):** The headline "12.200 cursos fora, 10.200 novos no lugar" highlights a significant change and a large number of courses being removed, which can trigger a sense of scarcity or the need to understand what's happening. The specific dates (2021-2025, 2024) add to this.
*   **Social Proof/Authority:** The mention of the "Ministério da Educação chinês" and the specific numbers (12.200, 10.200, 30%) lend significant authority to the information. The attribution to "Diego Almeida" also suggests a credible source.
*   **Curiosity/Intrigue:** The headline is designed to make the reader wonder *why* these courses are being suspended and what the "new" courses will entail. The phrase "reforma estrutural em curso" further piques curiosity.
*   **Relatability/Empathy:** The image of graduating students is universally relatable and evokes emotions associated with education, achievement, and the future. The selfie-taker makes it more contemporary and relatable to a younger audience.
*   **Visual Hierarchy:** The large, bold headline immediately draws attention. The body text is presented clearly below it. The image serves as a strong visual anchor, supporting the narrative.
*   **Contrast:** The use of white text on a dark background creates high contrast, making the text easily readable. The vibrant red robe of the selfie-taker also stands out against the more muted tones of the other students' attire.
*   **Navigation Cues:** The arrows and dots clearly indicate that this is an interactive element, encouraging users to explore further.
*   **Personalization/Trust:** The profile picture and name in the top right corner create a sense of a personal connection and build trust in the content provider.
