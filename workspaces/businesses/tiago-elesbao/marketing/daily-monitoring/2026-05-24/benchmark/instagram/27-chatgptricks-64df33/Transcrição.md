## 📝 Caption / Descrição original

2,194 likes, 112 comments - chatgptricks on May 22, 2026: "China is testing AI-powered robot barber kiosks.

Instead of sitting with a human barber, customers sit under a robotic arm that scans their head in 3D, analyzes face shape, hair texture, and desired style, then starts cutting automatically.

The claim is precision and lower cost, with some posts saying sessions can cost around ¥60.

It is a funny glimpse into where everyday automation is heading. Robots are no longer just working in factories or warehouses. They are moving into personal services too.

But this one feels different. Letting a machine sort packages is one thing. Letting it cut hair next to your head is another.

Would you trust a robot barber?
